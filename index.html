Read the dimensional assumptions +

The candidate assumes a 1200 × 1000 × 162 mm pallet and ten physical courses. Median model carton dimensions are about 143 × 149 × 141 mm. These are conditional fitted dimensions. Rear surfaces, support, and physical stability remain unverified.

UK and Euro pallet scales were both tested. The hidden rear edge prevents the fit from reliably identifying pallet type or occupied depth.
